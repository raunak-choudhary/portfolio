# Licences for the zodiac sky assets

This folder holds the images and data behind the admin dashboard's zodiac sky background. Every file shipped here is listed below with its author, source and licence.

## Constellation drawings (`art/*.webp`)

All eighteen drawings are cut from Sidney Hall's engravings for *Urania's Mirror; or, a View of the Heavens* (London, Samuel Leigh, 1824). The engravings are in the public domain worldwide: Hall died in 1831 and the work was published more than 100 years ago.

The scans come from Wikimedia Commons; the credits below are taken from each file's Commons metadata (fetched 2026-11-11). Most scans are restorations by Adam Cuerden of Library of Congress prints, Prints and Photographs Division. The files here are derived works: cropped, masked, converted to a white-on-transparent alpha map and resized to 720 px on the long side.

## Sun (`sun-uv.webp`)

The Sun in extreme ultraviolet (30.4 nm, false colour), imaged by the Atmospheric Imaging Assembly on NASA's Solar Dynamics Observatory, 19 August 2010. Courtesy of NASA/SDO and the AIA, EVE, and HMI science teams. Licence: public domain. Source: [The Sun by the Atmospheric Imaging Assembly, Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Sun_by_the_Atmospheric_Imaging_Assembly_of_NASA%27s_Solar_Dynamics_Observatory_-_20100819.jpg). Resized to 600 px.

This file is only a fallback. At runtime the dashboard loads NASA SDO's latest 30.4 nm image live from https://sdo.gsfc.nasa.gov/assets/img/latest/latest_1024_0304.jpg (NASA/SDO, public domain); that image is fetched by the viewer's browser and is not stored in this repository.

## Moon (`moon.webp`)

The Moon, NASA Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) imagery. Courtesy of NASA. Licence: public domain. Copied from the Earth-from-orbit preview capture; resized to 512 px. The black background is clipped away by the renderer.

Credit line used on the dashboard: "Sun and Moon: NASA".

## Constellation figures (`figures.json`)

`figures.json` is derived from two sources:

- [d3-celestial](https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial) by Olaf Frohn: constellation line figures and names. BSD 3-clause licence; the full licence text is in `LICENSE-d3-celestial.txt` in this folder.
- The Yale Bright Star Catalogue (5th revised edition, Hoffleit and Warren, 1991): star positions and magnitudes. Public domain.
